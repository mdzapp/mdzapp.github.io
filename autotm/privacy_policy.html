Privacy Policy for AutoTM

Last Updated: September 02, 2026

AutoTM ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you use the AutoTM mobile application.

1. Data Collection and Storage (Offline-First)

AutoTM is designed as an offline-first application. Most of your vehicle data, including maintenance logs, fuel records, expenses, and documents, are stored locally on your device using an encrypted local database.

  • Local Storage: We do not automatically upload your vehicle data to our servers.
  • User-Initiated Backup: If you use the "Export Backup" feature, a copy of your database is created. You are responsible for the security of where you choose to save or share this file (e.g., your own Google Drive or Email).

2. Permissions and Usage

To provide its core features, AutoTM requires the following permissions:

  • Location: Used to find nearby workshops, gas stations, and to save your last parking location. This data is processed on-device and is not shared with us.
  • Bluetooth: Required to scan for and connect to ELM327 OBD-II adapters for real-time vehicle diagnostics.
  • Camera/Storage: Used to take photos of receipts or documents and to attach files to your vehicle records.
  • Notifications: Used to send service reminders and document expiry alerts.

3. Third-Party Services

We use a limited number of third-party services to enhance app functionality:

  • RevenueCat: Used to manage premium subscriptions. RevenueCat may collect transaction data and an anonymous identifier to verify your purchase status.
  • Google Play Services: Used for app updates and basic crash reporting (if enabled on your device).

4. Data Retention and Deletion

You have full control over your data. You can delete all your information at any time using the "Clear All Data" option in the app settings. Uninstalling the app will also remove all locally stored data.

5. Children's Privacy

AutoTM is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
